OVERVIEW

RhythmParry is an independently conceptualized third-person action-combat project developed with a hybrid C++ and Blueprint workflow. Its central gameplay idea is an offensive parry mechanic built around timing and attack interactions.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Original concept and gameplay direction by ABHAY SINHA. I defined the mechanics and feature requirements, then implemented and integrated the systems with Epic Assistant used as a technical development aid.
